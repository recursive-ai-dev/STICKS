# GODFALL: ECHOES OF DELIRIUM

## World Overview
Welcome to the lore manifest of "Godfall: Echoes of Delirium," a dark fantasy world existing on the body of a dying god. This is a realm where reality fractures under the weight of collective delusion, where power is harvested from divine wounds, and where the line between sanity and madness has long since blurred beyond recognition.

In this brutal world, civilizations rise and fall upon the decaying flesh of a cosmic entity whose very nature corrupts those who dwell upon it. As the god slowly dies, its essence leaks into the world, twisting minds and bodies alike. Shamanic mages harvest this essence like oil, refining it for power while accelerating the world's descent into madness.

The inhabitants of this world suffer from generational psychosis, their perception of reality warped by hallucinations that have become cultural truths. What was once a unified global civilization has fractured into isolated regions where communication happens through fragmented dialects and symbolic writing, further isolating communities from shared reality.

## How to Use This Lore Manifest
This manifest is organized into a modular structure to allow for easy navigation and expansion. Each folder contains specific aspects of the world's lore, from core mythology to the manifestations of delusion across different regions.

### Directory Structure:
1. **Core Mythology** - The fundamental truths and myths about the dying god
2. **World Structure** - Physical and metaphysical composition of the world
3. **History** - Timeline of major events from creation to the current age
4. **Species** - Sentient beings and their evolutionary adaptations to divine corruption
5. **Factions** - Major power groups, their structures, and conflicts
6. **Magic System** - The extraction and use of god-essence as power
7. **Geography** - Regions, landmarks, and settlements across the god's body
8. **Language** - Fragmented communication systems and regional dialects
9. **Delusion Manifestations** - Catalog of hallucinations and mental deterioration
10. **Religion** - Belief systems and theological interpretations
11. **Artifacts** - Significant objects of power and cultural importance
12. **Notable Figures** - Key individuals throughout history and in the present

### Tick-Based Living Environment
This world operates on a "tick" system where the progression of delusion, the dying god's corruption, and faction activities all advance according to measurable intervals. This system allows for dynamic world evolution and can be used for gameplay mechanics or narrative progression.

## A Word of Caution
The lore contained within this manifest depicts a world of extreme darkness, psychological horror, and brutal survival. Themes of mental illness, religious fanaticism, body horror, and existential dread permeate the material. This content is intended for mature audiences interested in exploring the depths of dark fantasy worldbuilding.

Welcome to Godfall, where madness is clarity and the only truth is delusion.
