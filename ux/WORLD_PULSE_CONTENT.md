# Microcopy & Content Pack

## Constraints
- **Tone:** dark, gothic, delusional
- **Avoid List:** game over, points, winner
- **Localization:** en, la, gr

### Context: notifications
#### Informational
- **Variation 1:** A NEW DELUSION HAS TAKEN ROOT.
- **Variation 2:** THE GOD'S PULSE APPROACHES.
- **Variation 3:** REALITY IS SHIFTING.
#### Celebratory
- **Variation 1:** YOUR LIMBS HAVE ASCENDED.
- **Variation 2:** CHAOS THRIVES IN YOUR WAKE.
- **Variation 3:** SANITY LOST! GLORY GAINED!
#### Error
- **Variation 1:** DIVINE FRACTURE PREVENTED THIS ACTION.
- **Variation 2:** SANITY LEVELS TOO HIGH TO PROCEED.
- **Variation 3:** THE VOID REFUSES YOUR REQUEST.
#### Warning
- **Variation 1:** YOUR REALITY IS FRAYING AT THE EDGES.
- **Variation 2:** LIMBS ARE REACHING CRITICAL TENSION.
- **Variation 3:** THE GOD'S PULSE IS IMMINENT.

**Negative-Prompt List (To Avoid Repetition):**
- Don't use game over phrasing.
- Avoid starting every sentence with 'You'.
